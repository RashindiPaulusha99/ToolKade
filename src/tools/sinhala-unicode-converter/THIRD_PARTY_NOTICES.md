# Third-party notices

`fm-abhaya.json` (FM Abhaya → Unicode mapping) and `fm-abhaya.test-cases.json` (test corpus) are copied
unmodified from **Pandukabhaya** by akuruAI: https://github.com/akuruAI/pandukabhaya
(`pandukabhaya/mappings/fm_abhaya.json` and `tests/test_cases.json`). The mapping is itself a corrected
version of the mapping published by the Language Technology Research Laboratory, University of Colombo
School of Computing.

The Unicode → FM Abhaya conventions in `unicode-to-fm.ts` are ToolKade's own. They use only glyph codes
that the Pandukabhaya mapping decodes back to the same Unicode text, and are tested by round trip.

FM Abhaya is a proprietary font by Pushpananda Ekanayaka. It is not distributed with this site.
